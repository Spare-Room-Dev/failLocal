# Plan 2b: rulings made during the build

The Plan 2b build ([plan](2026-10-27-faillocal-site-2b.md)) ran without stopping for questions. Where the plan was ambiguous, wrong or in conflict with itself, the controller made a ruling and recorded it as "what was decided — why — what it costs if wrong". This file keeps those rulings after the build workspace was deleted on 28 Oct 2026. Decisions with lasting effect on the product are also in `docs/decisions.md` (D-104 to D-112).

## Rulings

- R-a: "buildSceneState" in Task 1/global text means the existing `sceneState(r, sample)` builder — no rename — wrong costs nothing.
- R-b: add `pretest`, `pretypecheck` (if a site typecheck script exists) and `predev`/`prebuild` hooks that run build-register.mjs; the generated JSON stays gitignored — keeps fresh clones working — if wrong, a stale JSON could be tested; the deep-equal test catches drift.
- R-c: the "site bundle has no yaml parser" check lives in check-build.mjs (with a unit test of the check function on fixture strings), not a vitest test that needs a build — tests stay fast — wrong costs a weaker test.
- R-d: haul lanes are ±4.6 m either side of the road centreline (9.2 m centre-to-centre) on 26 m roads; the test asserts 9.2 m separation — 4.6 m apart would overlap 7.6 m-wide trucks — wrong costs a constant change.
- R-e: "north rim / west to east" = entry posts sit along the board's back (far, top-of-screen) edge region and are ordered by projected screen x to match SERVICE_GROUPS order — cards are laid out in screen x — wrong costs a layout tweak.
- R-f: roads may terminate at a structure's pad edge; the test checks road centrelines do not cross a footprint interior except at declared endpoints — otherwise the invariant is unsatisfiable — wrong costs a test tweak.
- R-g: Task 5's DioramaScene ships the minimal accessible DOM contract layer (area buttons at projected anchors, .scene-plinth, data-cable-group markers, onAnchors) and joins the contract describe.each in Task 5; Task 7 refines (camera-following positions, plate pinning at zoom, card centring tolerances, phone crop, welcome hero) — keeps every commit green — wrong costs some T7 rework.
- R-h: "train-moving" in the still-frame test is derived from scene.train.speed > 0; AnimState gets no extra field — wrong costs nothing.
- R-i: Task 9 is controller-orchestrated: an implementer captures screenshots and runs the detector; the controller dispatches impeccable-finish-reviewer and impeccable-documenter; an implementer applies the fix batch — subagents may not spawn subagents — wrong costs nothing.
- R-j: port 4173 is held by an unrelated user process (python http.server for BUZZD); never kill it. Task 1 adds an E2E_PORT env override (default 4173) to site/playwright.config.ts; implementers run e2e with E2E_PORT=4183 — wrong costs a one-line config.
- R-k: contract wording = "element with button semantics"; SVG schematic keeps <g role=button>; HTML layers (diorama overlay) must use native <button>; Task 5 adds a per-implementation nativeButtons flag to the contract describe.each and amends the SceneView.tsx contract comment — native buttons inside SVG would need foreignObject for no a11y gain — wrong costs a small test change.
- R-l: replace the plan's "25% of bounding box" rule with a world-space clamp: inverse-project the view centre to the ground, clamp it into the board [0,w]x[0,h], re-derive ox/oy; k clamped to [K_MIN,K_MAX]. Test inverse-projects the view and asserts board is visible (view centre on the board) across the k/origin sweep — the plan's wording was the defect; intent is "camera can't be lost" — wrong costs a small camera tweak.
- R-m: compass is screen-based (north = up screen/back of board) — the plan's compass words were undefined — wrong costs a layout re-arrangement.
- R-n: a cable "anchor" is where the cable enters the stage top under its card — the card slots stay at cableFraction(i); each cable drops from its anchor and routes octilinearly (9 px chamfers) to its entry post (DESIGN: "octilinear, from the stage top down to the posts"). onAnchors reports those stage-top x fractions; data-cable-group markers sit at the anchors. T6 draws the routes; T7's card-centring test checks cards vs anchors — entry posts project ~78 px apart while cards are ~147 px wide, so cards can't sit over posts — wrong costs T6/T7 rework of cable routing.
- R-o: the canvas "100 m" scale bar is a map annotation of display geometry (D-105), not a model quantity; it stays outside <Qty> — DESIGN mandates it and it makes no claim about the site's performance — wrong costs wrapping it in a tagged DOM label.
- R-p: entry posts move into the default framing — a row of entry posts in the north band of LAYOUT.defaultFocus (e.g. along the north LV/site road near the comms/admin end), still ordered by screen x to match SERVICE_GROUPS, so every cable visibly reaches its post at the default desktop view; owned by Task 7 (layout test updated) — zooming out to the rim would shrink the working area too far — wrong costs a layout tweak.
- R-q: plaque placement is a Task 7 requirement: at 1440, 1150, 1024 and 820 default views, no two plaques overlap and none is clipped by the stage edge (collision-aware placement with leader to its flag pole; e2e bounding-box check) — current plaques are tuned to one view and collide — wrong costs a placement tweak.
- R-r: light vehicles move only while haulage is not stopped (the haulage process "keeps autonomous trucks, crewed vehicles and people apart" — when it stops, crewed vehicles in the mine area are stood down); otherwise they park; still frame shows them parked/still — honours the "motion only while bound state runs" constraint — wrong costs a one-line binding change.
- R-s: the static dot on a live cable in reduced motion stays (it marks "live" without motion; brief's "no dots" meant no travelling dots) — wrong costs removing one draw.
- R-t: a revoked service's entry post is greyed like a cut one (revoked access is cut for the site) — wrong costs one condition.
- R-u: reasoning prose inside a provenance popover IS the provenance of its tagged value; numbers in it are quotations traceable through the cited decision ref. Exempt only the reasoning text node (e.g. [data-reasoning]), not the whole tag wrapper, and require (by test) that every exempted reasoning string carries a D-ref; all other text in the popover/wrapper stays scanned. Spelling digits out to dodge the scanner is not allowed — the reviewer's "fix the four strings" would game the check without adding honesty — wrong costs tagging four reasoning quotes later.
- R-v: day-13 US$0 is fixed by presentation (stockpile state line + computed per-day rate), no scenario/engine change — wrong costs a scenario tweak.
- R-w: no walkthrough-video button until the video exists (dead control); video added to owner gates — wrong costs one button.
- R-x: "Show the network" overlay deferred out of 2b (feature, not finish fix); D-110 + Limitations line — wrong costs building the overlay in a later plan.
- R-y: finish review capped at two rounds per plan; the round-2 blocker plus cheap polish (rate chain merge, speed labels, hero shadow edge, card row height, status indent) go into one final fix dispatch together with the code-review findings, verified by screenshots + scoped re-review, not a third design round — wrong costs a small follow-up polish.
- R-z: count to the low mark ("N days before loadings are cut back") and state both rates (half at cut-back, full at empty), from existing tagged inputs; show only while shipping normal AND stockpile falling — the engine degrades rail at the low mark, so "runs dry" overstated the buffer — wrong costs a copy/condition change.
- R-aa: "Modelled on" only for the scenario whose basis is the incident record it reproduces (s1); others "Based on" — a survey/architecture isn't an incident a scenario is modelled on — wrong costs a word.
- R-ab: wagons fill while the train runs (speed > 0), belt stays bound to conveyor — the loadout is fed from the stockpile, so plan:360/D-108 were wrong — wrong costs a binding change.
- R-ac: countdown = the run's actual time until the stockpile crosses the low mark (from future samples); hidden if it never happens in the run, rate line likewise — "days of stock" presented as time was false — wrong costs a copy change.
